### Overview
TheaterSplit ("we", "our", or "extension") is developed by sinary.org. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when using the TheaterSplit extension for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

### Data Collection and Usage
**TheaterSplit does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, watch history, comments, or browsing activity.**

- **YouTube Host Access (`https://www.youtube.com/*`, `https://youtube.com/*`)**:  
  Used strictly inside your browser to rearrange the YouTube watch page layout so the video stays on the left and comments appear in a scrollable panel on the right. TheaterSplit never reads, logs, or transmits video IDs, comments, or account details to any external server.

- **Storage Permission (`storage`)**:  
  Used exclusively via your browser's native storage API (`chrome.storage.sync`) to save your local layout preferences (such as split panel width, default tab, live stream auto-skip, and shortcut toggles).

- **Scripting Permission (`scripting`)**:  
  Used exclusively on YouTube tabs to activate TheaterSplit on already-open YouTube watch tabs right after installation or when triggering a keyboard shortcut, without requiring a manual page reload.

### Third-Party Services
TheaterSplit contains zero analytics, zero tracking cookies, zero advertising SDKs, and makes zero outbound network requests. All layout adjustments run 100% locally in your browser.
